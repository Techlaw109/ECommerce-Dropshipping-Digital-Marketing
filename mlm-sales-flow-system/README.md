## 🗺️ The Complete Sales Flow

This is your daily operating system. Follow it every day:

1. **📝 Post Your Hand-Raiser** (5 mins)
   - Choose one template from Module 1
   - Customize to your niche
   - Post on your highest-reach platform
   - Include keyword trigger

2. **🔥 Post Your Teaser** (5 mins)
   - Share curiosity-building post from Module 2
   - Supports your hand-raiser
   - Schedule for different time of day

3. **💬 Reply to Comments & DMs FAST** (15 mins)
   - Respond within 5 minutes
   - Start with Warm Welcome
   - Speed = conversion

4. **🔍 Run the DM Conversation Flow** (20 mins per prospect)
   - Follow 6-step framework
   - Don't skip steps
   - Let conversation breathe

5. **🎁 Present Your First-Result Offer** (5 mins)
   - Share entry-level offer
   - Make it low-risk, high-value
   - Include urgency + support promise

6. **✅ Collect Payment / Signup** (2 mins)
   - Send GCash/PayMaya or MLM sign-up link
   - Confirm commitment
   - Schedule onboarding call

7. **🔁 Follow Up & Compound** (Daily)
   - Follow up with warm prospects
   - Onboard new sign-ups
   - Teach them this flow
   - Repeat daily
