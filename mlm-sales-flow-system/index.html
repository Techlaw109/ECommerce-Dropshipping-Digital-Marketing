Module 1

🎯 Hand-Raiser Campaign

Turn your social media into a prospect-identifying machine — without spending a single peso on ads.

What Is a Hand-Raiser Campaign?

A hand-raiser campaign is a simple post or story that asks your audience to take a small action (like commenting a keyword or sending a DM) if they're interested in a specific result. It filters out the curious from the committed — so you only spend time talking to people who already raised their hand.

Instead of cold-messaging 100 people and getting blocked, you post ONE hand-raiser and let the interested people come to you. This is how top earners in network marketing build their pipeline — they never chase, they attract and filter.
People don't want to be sold to — they want to be heard. A hand-raiser gives them permission to express interest without commitment. It shifts the dynamic from you pushing to them pulling.
"Who else wants to [specific result] without [common pain point]? Comment 'INTERESTED' below and I'll send you the exact steps I used. 🔥" — Simple, direct, and it works in ANY network marketing niche.
Facebook personal profile (highest reach), Facebook stories, Instagram stories, TikTok with keyword trigger, Viber/WhatsApp broadcast lists. Start where your audience already is.

📝 Plug & Play Hand-Raiser Templates

Template A: The "Who Else" Hand-Raiser
Who else wants to earn an extra [₱10K-₱50K/month] without quitting their job?
I just opened a few spots for people who want to learn how I did it — step by step.
No fancy tech needed. No big capital. Just a phone and the willingness to follow a proven system.
Comment "CURIOUS" or DM me the word "FLOW" and I'll send you the details. 🔥
💡 MLM Tip: Replace the income range with something realistic for YOUR market. Don't overpromise — build trust from the first interaction.
Template B: The "Pain Point" Hand-Raiser
Real talk: Are you tired of posting every day but nothing's converting?
I was there too. Posting. Hashtagging. Hoping. But no sales.
Then I learned one thing that changed everything: It's not about posting more — it's about having a FLOW.
If you want to see the exact flow I now use to close deals without ads, DM me "SYSTEM" and I'll share it with you.
💡 MLM Tip: This works because it addresses the exact pain your fellow network marketers feel. They become curious because you're describing THEIR problem.
Template C: The "Result Story" Hand-Raiser
[Name] was skeptical. No sales experience. Zero network. But she followed one simple system...
In 7 days, she had her first 3 customers. In 30 days, she hit [rank/bonus level].
No ads. No complex funnel. Just a clear flow from post → message → payment.
Want to know what she did? Comment "HOW" below and I'll walk you through it. 👇
💡 MLM Tip: Use a real story from your team (with permission). Social proof is the most powerful trigger in network marketing. Real people, real results.
✅

Hand-Raiser Campaign Checklist

  • Choose ONE specific resultWhat transformation are you promising? (e.g., "First sale in 7 days" or "Extra ₱10K this month")
  • Identify the pain pointWhat's the frustration your prospect feels RIGHT NOW? (e.g., "Posting daily but no one's buying")
  • Choose a keyword triggerPick one word they can comment or DM: "CURIOUS", "HOW", "FLOW", "INTERESTED", "SYSTEM"
  • Write your hand-raiser postUse one of the templates above. Keep it under 6 lines. End with a clear CTA.
  • Post at peak engagement timeFor Philippine market: 7-9 AM, 12-1 PM, or 7-9 PM. Test and track.
  • Respond within 5 minutesWhen someone comments or DMs, reply FAST. Speed = conversion.
  • Track your numbersHow many saw? How many engaged? How many DM'd? How many closed? Improve each week.

🔥 Teaser Content Strategy

Create curiosity that pulls people in — instead of pushing your opportunity on people who aren't ready.

❌ What Doesn't Work

  • ✕
    "Join my MLM! Best opportunity ever!"
  • ✕
    Posting product prices and waiting for buyers
  • ✕
    Sharing the compensation plan in full upfront
  • ✕
    Before/after product photos with no story

✅ What Works (Teaser Approach)

  • ✓
    Share a RESULT without revealing the method yet
  • ✓
    Tell a STORY that makes people want to know more
  • ✓
    Ask a QUESTION that reveals their pain
  • ✓
    Give a MICRO-INSIGHT that proves your expertise

💡 The Curiosity Formula

Result + Mystery + Invitation = Teaser

"I just hit [result] using a method most people overlook. It has nothing to do with [common misconception]. If you want to know what it IS... DM me 'TELL ME' and I'll share."

📝 Teaser Content Templates for Network Marketing

Teaser Type 1: The "Behind the Curtain" Post
People keep asking me how I [specific result].
Here's what I won't do: I won't post 10x a day. I won't slide into your DMs with a pitch. I won't spend on ads.
Here's what I WILL do: I follow a simple 4-step flow that turns curiosity into closed deals.
Want to see what's behind the curtain? DM me "SHOW ME" 👀
💡 This works because it addresses objections BEFORE they arise. You're showing what you DON'T do (which they hate about MLM) and hinting at a system (which they want).
Teaser Type 2: The "One Thing" Story
One thing changed everything for me in this business.
Before: Posting daily, zero sales, zero signups.
After: People come to ME asking how to join. I don't chase — I attract.
The shift wasn't about working harder. It was about having ONE thing in place.
If you want to know what that one thing is... comment "ONE" below 👇
💡 This teaser is powerful for MLM because it mirrors your prospect's current situation AND promises a simple solution. Simple = believable.
Teaser Type 3: The "Small Proof" Post
Just got this message from [team member name]:
"Coach, I just closed my 3rd deal this week using the flow you taught me! 🙏"
She started with zero experience 14 days ago.
No ads. No big following. Just a simple flow and the guts to follow it.
If someone on your team could use this same flow... send them my way. DM "FLOW" and I'll share. 🔥
💡 Social proof + curiosity = irresistible. Make sure you have permission to share testimonials. Real screenshots are even more powerful.

💬 DM Conversation Framework

The exact conversation flow that closes deals without pressure — from the first DM to "Where do I send payment?"

1

👋 The Warm Welcome

Acknowledge their action. Thank them. Make them feel seen. Never jump into a pitch.

2

🔍 The Discovery Question

Ask ONE question to understand their situation. "What made you interested?" or "What's your biggest challenge right now?"

3

👂 The Listen & Validate

Repeat back what they said in your own words. Show you understand. Build trust before you ever mention your offer.

4

🎯 The Bridge

Connect their pain/desire to your solution. "What if I told you there's a way to [their desired result] without [their biggest fear]?"

5

🎁 The First-Result Offer

Present your entry-level offer — something quick, low-risk, and high-value. Not the full opportunity yet. Just the first step.

₱

✅ The Close (Natural)

"Ready to try it? Here's where you start: [payment/signup link]. I'll personally guide you through the first step."

📝 Complete DM Conversation Script (MLM-Specific)

Full DM Close: From "CURIOUS" to Payment
YOU: Hey [Name]! Thanks for commenting on my post 👋 Saw you're interested in [topic/result]. What specifically caught your attention?
⏱ Wait for their reply. Don't double-text. Patience = power.
THEM: [They share their reason — listen carefully!]
YOU: I hear you, [Name]. That's actually super common. A lot of people feel [repeat their pain in your words]. Here's the thing though — what if there was a way to [their desired result] without having to [their biggest fear/objection]?
⏱ Wait for reply. They'll usually say "How?" or "Tell me more" — that's the green light.
THEM: How?? / Tell me more! / What is it?
YOU: So I'm part of a community that helps people [specific result]. We have a simple system — no tech headaches, no big investment, and you don't need to be "salesy." The first step is [your first-result offer — e.g., a free intro session, a ₱297 starter kit, a 3-day challenge]. Would you be open to checking it out?
⏱ This is the bridge. You're not pitching the whole MLM — you're inviting them to a LOW-RISK first step.
THEM: Sure / What is it? / How much?
YOU: [Share the first-result offer details + price if applicable]. It's designed to give you a quick win — and from there, you'll see for yourself if it's a fit. Either way, you'll walk away with [specific value]. Want me to send you the link?
THEM: Yes / Send it / Ok
YOU: Here you go: [LINK] 🔥 After you sign up, send me a screenshot and I'll personally walk you through the first step. You won't be doing this alone — I've got you. 💪
🎯 KEY: Always offer personal support after the close. This is what separates you from every other MLM rep who just sends a link and disappears. Follow-up is where the fortune is.

Most MLMs tell you to 'make a list of 100 names' and just talk to everyone. That's not a system — that's a recipe for burnout. What I'm showing you is a completely different approach. Would you be open to seeing the difference?"
