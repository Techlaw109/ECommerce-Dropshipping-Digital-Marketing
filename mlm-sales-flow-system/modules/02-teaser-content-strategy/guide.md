# Module 2: Teaser Content Strategy 🔥

## Why Most MLM Content Doesn't Work

Most network marketers post:
- "Join my MLM! Best opportunity ever!"
- Product prices and waiting for buyers
- The full compensation plan upfront
- Before/after product photos with no story

These don't work because they trigger resistance. People feel sold to, and their guard goes up. Teaser content works because it creates CURIOSITY — and curiosity pulls people in instead of pushing them away.

---

## The Curiosity Formula

**Result + Mystery + Invitation = Teaser**

"I just hit [result] using a method most people overlook. It has nothing to do with [common misconception]. If you want to know what it IS... DM me 'TELL ME' and I'll share."

---

## Teaser Type 1: The "Behind the Curtain" Post

```
People keep asking me how I [specific result].

Here's what I won't do: I won't post 10x a day. I won't slide into your DMs with a pitch. I won't spend on ads.

Here's what I WILL do: I follow a simple 4-step flow that turns curiosity into closed deals.

Want to see what's behind the curtain? DM me "SHOW ME" 👀
```

**Why it works:** It addresses objections BEFORE they arise. You're showing what you DON'T do (which they hate about MLM) and hinting at a system (which they want).

---

## Teaser Type 2: The "One Thing" Story

```
One thing changed everything for me in this business.

Before: Posting daily, zero sales, zero signups.
After: People come to ME asking how to join. I don't chase — I attract.

The shift wasn't about working harder. It was about having ONE thing in place.

If you want to know what that one thing is... comment "ONE" below 👇
```

**Why it works:** It mirrors your prospect's current situation AND promises a simple solution. Simple = believable.

---

## Teaser Type 3: The "Small Proof" Post

```
Just got this message from [team member name]:

"Coach, I just closed my 3rd deal this week using the flow you taught me! 🙏"

She started with zero experience 14 days ago.

No ads. No big following. Just a simple flow and the guts to follow it.

If someone on your team could use this same flow... send them my way. DM "FLOW" and I'll share. 🔥
```

**Why it works:** Social proof + curiosity = irresistible. Use real testimonials (with permission). Screenshots are even more powerful.

## Teaser Content Rules

1. **Never reveal the full solution in the post** — That's what the DM is for
2. **Always end with a clear CTA** — "DM me [KEYWORD]" or "Comment [KEYWORD]"
3. **One teaser per day** — Don't overwhelm your audience
4. **Alternate hand-raisers and teasers** — Balance attraction and curiosity
5. **Use real stories whenever possible** — Social proof beats clever copy
6. **Keep it under 6 lines** — Shorter = more curiosity
7. **Don't mention your MLM company name** — Save that for the DM conversation

---

## Content Pairing Strategy

For maximum results, pair your content:

**Morning (7-9 AM):** Post a hand-raiser
**Afternoon (12-1 PM) or Evening (7-9 PM):** Post a teaser

This creates two touchpoints per day and keeps you visible without being spammy. The hand-raiser catches the early birds, the teaser catches the evening scrollers.

---

## Measuring Teaser Effectiveness

Track these metrics for each post:

- **Reach/Views:** How many people saw it
- **Engagement Rate:** Comments, likes, shares
- **DM Conversion:** How many DM'd you per post
- **Keyword Usage:** How many used your specific keyword
- **Close Rate:** How many DMs turned into sales

Double down on the teaser type that gets the highest DM conversion rate.
