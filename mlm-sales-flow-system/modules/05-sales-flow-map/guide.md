# Sales Flow Map 🗺️

## The Complete Daily Operating System

This is your daily flow. Follow it every day and the results compound. Print this out and keep it next to your phone.

---

## Step 1: Post Your Hand-Raiser (5 mins) 📝

**Action:** Choose one template from Module 1. Customize it to your niche. Post it on your highest-reach platform.

**Details:**
- Pick Template A, B, or C based on what worked best this week
- Customize the bracketed text with YOUR specifics
- Include a keyword trigger (e.g., "Comment FLOW")
- Post during peak hours: 7-9 AM, 12-1 PM, or 7-9 PM PHT
- Don't post more than one hand-raiser per day

**Success Metric:** 5+ comments or DMs per post

---

## Step 2: Post Your Teaser (5 mins) 🔥

**Action:** Share a curiosity-building post from Module 2. Schedule it for a different time of day than your hand-raiser.

**Details:**
- Rotate between Type 1, 2, and 3 teasers
- Always end with a DM CTA + keyword
- Keep it under 6 lines
- Don't reveal your solution in the post
- Use real stories whenever possible

**Success Metric:** 3+ DMs per teaser post

---

## Step 3: Reply to Comments & DMs FAST (15 mins) 💬

**Action:** When someone comments your keyword or DMs you, respond within 5 minutes.

**Details:**
- Start with the Warm Welcome (Step 1 of DM Framework)
- Use their first name
- Reference their specific action
- End with a question — never a pitch
- Speed = conversion

**Success Metric:** 100% response rate within 5 minutes

---

## Step 4: Run the DM Conversation Flow (20 mins per prospect) 🔍

**Action:** Follow the 6-step DM framework from Module 3.

**Details:**
1. 👋 Warm Welcome
2. 🔍 Discovery Question
3. 👂 Listen & Validate
4. 🎯 The Bridge
5. 🎁 First-Result Offer
6. ✅ The Close (Natural)

- Don't skip steps
- Don't rush — let the conversation breathe
- Wait for their reply before moving to the next step
- Have your objection scripts ready
- Always offer personal support after the close

**Success Metric:** 50% of DMs reach Step 5 (offer presentation)

---

## Step 5: Present Your First-Result Offer (5 mins) 🎁

**Action:** Share your entry-level offer from Module 4.

**Details:**
- Make it low-risk and high-value
- Include urgency element
- Include your personal support promise
- Don't oversell — let the offer speak for itself
- End with "Want me to send you the link?"

**Success Metric:** 30% of offer presentations result in a "yes"

---

## Step 6: Collect Payment / Signup (2 mins) ✅

**Action:** Send your GCash/PayMaya link or MLM sign-up link.

**Details:**
- Confirm their commitment verbally first
- Send the payment/signup link
- Confirm when payment is received
- Schedule their onboarding/welcome call immediately
- Add them to your team group chat

**Success Metric:** 80% of "yes" responses complete payment within 24 hours

---

## Step 7: Follow Up & Compound (Daily) 🔁

**Action:** Follow up with prospects who haven't closed yet. Onboard new sign-ups. Teach them this same flow.

**Details:**
- 80% of sales happen after 5+ follow-ups
- Use the follow-up sequence from Module 3
- Onboard new team members using the Team Playbook (Module 6)
- Teach them to teach others (duplication = exponential growth)
- Repeat this entire flow daily

**Success Metric:** 3+ follow-ups per warm prospect per week

## Key Numbers to Track

- **Posts per week:** How many hand-raisers + teasers you posted
- **DMs received:** How many people reached out
- **Conversations started:** How many DM conversations you initiated
- **Offers presented:** How many times you reached Step 5
- **Closes:** How many deals you closed
- **Conversion rate:** Closes ÷ Conversations started
- **Revenue per conversation:** Total revenue ÷ Conversations started

Track these weekly. What gets measured gets improved.
