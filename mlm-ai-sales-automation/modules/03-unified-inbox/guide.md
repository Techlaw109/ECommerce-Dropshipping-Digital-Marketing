# Module 3: Unified Inbox

## Overview

The Unified Inbox consolidates all your communications into one dashboard. No more switching between email, SMS, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, and other platforms - everything is in one place.

## What You'll Learn

- How to connect all communication channels
- How to organize and prioritize messages
- How to use team collaboration features
- How to automate message routing and responses

## Why This Matters

Communication fragmentation kills sales. When messages are scattered across multiple platforms, you miss opportunities, respond slowly, and provide poor customer experience. The Unified Inbox solves this by bringing everything together.

## Step 1: Connect Communication Channels

### Supported Channels

- Email (Gmail, Outlook, custom domains)
- SMS/MMS (Twilio, TextMagic, etc.)
- Facebook Messenger
- Instagram DMs
- LinkedIn Messages
- WhatsApp Business

### Connection Process

2. Click "Add Channel"

- Connect SMS provider

- Configure auto-replies
- Enable compliance features

**Social Media:**
- Connect business accounts
- Enable message monitoring
- Set up quick replies
- Configure notification settings

## Step 4: Automate Message Handling

### Auto-Responses

Set up automatic responses for common scenarios:

**New Lead Auto-Response:**
```
"Thanks for reaching out! I've received your message and will get back to you within 24 hours. In the meantime, feel free to check out [LINK] for more information. Looking forward to connecting!"
```

**After Hours Auto-Response:**
```
"Thanks for your message! I'm currently outside business hours but will respond as soon as I'm back. For urgent matters, please call [PHONE]. Otherwise, expect a response by [TIME]."
```

**Away Auto-Response:**
```
"Thanks for reaching out! I'm currently away until [DATE] with limited access to messages. I'll respond as soon as I return. For urgent matters, please contact [TEAM MEMBER]."
```

### Message Routing

Automatically route messages to the right person:

**Route by Channel:**
- Facebook messages → Social Media Manager
- Email inquiries → Sales Team
- Support requests → Customer Service
- Team questions → Upline/Leader

**Route by Topic:**
- Product questions → Product Specialist
- Compensation questions → Business Coach
- Technical issues → Tech Support
- General inquiries → Available team member

**Route by Priority:**
- Urgent messages → Immediate notification
- High priority → Top of queue
- Normal priority → Standard queue
- Low priority → Batch processing

## Step 5: Use Quick Replies

### Create Quick Reply Templates

**Category: Greetings**
```
"Hi {name}! Thanks for reaching out. How can I help you today?"
"Hello! Great to hear from you. What's on your mind?"
"Hey there! Thanks for your message. What can I do for you?"
```

**Category: Common Questions**
```
"Our products are [DESCRIPTION]. You can learn more at [LINK]."
"The compensation plan works like this: [EXPLANATION]."
"To get started, you'll need to [STEPS]. More info: [LINK]."
```

**Category: Scheduling**
```
"I'd love to chat! I'm available at [TIMES]. Which works for you?"
"Let's schedule a call! Here are my available times: [TIMES]."
"How about [TIME] for a quick call? Let me know if that works!"
```

**Category: Closing**
```
"Great chatting with you! I'll follow up on [DATE]."
"Thanks for your time! Looking forward to connecting again soon."
"Have an amazing day! Don't hesitate to reach out if you need anything."
```

### Use Quick Replies Efficiently

**Keyboard Shortcuts:**
- Press "/" to open quick reply menu
- Type to search templates
- Press Enter to insert
- Customize with variables

**Personalization:**
- Use {name} for recipient's name
- Use {your_name} for your name
- Use {company} for company name
- Use custom variables as needed

## Best Practices

### Do's

✅ Check inbox regularly throughout the day  
✅ Respond to urgent messages immediately  
✅ Use templates for consistency  
✅ Keep responses concise and clear  
✅ Add context with internal notes  
✅ Follow up on outstanding messages  
✅ Maintain professional tone  
✅ Respect customer's preferred channel  

### Don'ts

❌ Don't leave messages unread for too long  
❌ Don't use generic responses without personalization  
❌ Don't ignore messages from any channel  
❌ Don't duplicate responses (check if team already responded)  
❌ Don't be overly formal or robotic  
❌ Don't forget to follow up  
❌ Don't mix personal and business messages  
❌ Don't violate privacy or compliance rules
