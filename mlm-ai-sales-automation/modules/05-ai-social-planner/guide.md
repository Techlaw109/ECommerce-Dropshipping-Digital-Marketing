# Module 5: AI Social Planner

## Overview

The AI Social Planner generates hundreds of on-brand social media posts tailored to your goals. It creates content, schedules posts, and ensures your social media presence is consistent and engaging - all powered by artificial intelligence.

## What You'll Learn

- How to configure your AI content settings
- How to generate content for different platforms
- How to create a 30-day content calendar
- How to use MLM-specific content templates

## Why This Matters

Content creation is the biggest time sink for network marketers. You need to post consistently to stay visible, but creating content takes hours. The AI Social Planner automates this process, generating engaging posts in minutes instead of hours.

## Step 1: Configure Your AI Content Settings

### Brand Profile

Set up your brand profile for consistent content:

**Personal Information:**
- Your name and title
- Your MLM company name
- Your team name
- Your unique value proposition
- Your target audience description

**Brand Voice:**
- Tone: Professional / Casual / Enthusiastic / Educational
- Style: Storytelling / Direct / Inspirational / Humorous
- Values: Family / Freedom / Growth / Community / Health
- Keywords: Words to include regularly
- Avoid: Topics or words to never use

**Content Pillars:**
Choose 3-5 content pillars that define your content:
1. **Lifestyle** - Time freedom, travel, family life
2. **Education** - Tips, how-tos, industry insights
3. **Social Proof** - Testimonials, results, transformations
4. **Personal** - Behind the scenes, personal stories
5. **Opportunity** - Business sharing, recruiting content

## Step 3: Create a 30-Day Content Calendar

### Content Distribution

Use the 80/20 rule:
- 80% value content (tips, stories, education)
- 20% promotional content (opportunity, products, offers)

### Weekly Content Schedule

**Monday: Motivation**
- Inspirational quote or story
- Mindset content
- Weekly goals/intentions

**Tuesday: Education**
- Tips and how-tos
- Industry insights
- Training content

**Wednesday: Social Proof**
- Testimonials
- Team wins
- Before/after stories

**Thursday: Behind the Scenes**
- Daily routine
- Work-from-home life
- Personal moments

**Friday: Lifestyle**
- Freedom moments
- Travel/adventures
- Family time

**Saturday: Engagement**
- Questions
- Polls
- Challenges

**Sunday: Reflection**
- Weekly recap
- Gratitude posts
- Looking ahead

### 30-Day AI Content Generation

Use the AI to generate your entire month of content:

1. Open AI Social Planner
2. Select "Generate 30-Day Calendar"
3. Choose your content pillars
4. Select platforms
5. Click "Generate"
6. Review and customize each post
7. Schedule all posts at once

## Step 5: Schedule and Automate

### Scheduling Best Practices

**Best Times to Post:**

Facebook:
- Best: Monday-Wednesday, 12-3 PM
- Good: Thursday-Friday, 1-4 PM
- Avoid: Weekends (unless community-building)

Instagram:
- Best: Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM-12 PM
- Good: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 11 AM-1 PM
- Reels: Any day, 9-11 AM

LinkedIn:
- Best: Tuesday-Thursday, 7-8 AM, 12 PM, 5-6 PM
- Good: Monday-Wednesday, 8-10 AM
- Avoid: Weekends

### Automation Rules

1. Generate content in batches (weekly or monthly)
2. Schedule all posts at once
3. Check daily for engagement to respond to
4. Adjust based on performance data
5. Refresh content monthly

## Best Practices

### Do's

✅ Create content in batches  
✅ Use a mix of content types  
✅ Include personal stories and experiences  
✅ Engage with comments on your posts  
✅ Test different formats and topics  
✅ Stay consistent with posting schedule  
✅ Use relevant hashtags  
✅ Include clear call-to-actions  

### Don'ts

❌ Don't post only promotional content  
❌ Don't use stock photos exclusively  
❌ Don't ignore engagement on your posts  
❌ Don't post without checking for errors  
❌ Don't copy other people's content  
❌ Don't post the same thing across all platforms  
❌ Don't forget to be authentic and genuine  
❌ Don't violate company compliance guidelines
