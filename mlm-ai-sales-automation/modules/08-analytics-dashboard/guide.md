## Step 2: Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Lead Generation KPIs

**Daily Active Leads:**
- Target: 5-10 new leads per day
- Measure: Unique leads entering your system
- Track by: Source, channel, campaign

**Lead Source ROI:**
- Measure: Cost per lead by source
- Compare: Organic vs. paid vs. referral
- Optimize: Focus on highest ROI sources

**Engagement Rate:**
- Target: 5-10% engagement on social posts
- Measure: Likes, comments, shares, saves
- Track by: Post type, time, platform

### Conversion KPIs

**DM Response Rate:**
- Target: 60-80% of DMs get responses
- Measure: Replies received / DMs sent
- Track by: Template, timing, channel

**Qualification Rate:**
- Target: 40-60% of leads qualify
- Measure: Qualified leads / Total leads
- Track by: Source, AI bot accuracy

**Appointment Show Rate:**
- Target: 70-80% of booked appointments show up
- Measure: Appointments attended / Appointments booked
- Track by: Time of day, day of week, channel

**Close Rate:**
- Target: 20-30% of appointments result in enrollment
- Measure: Enrollments / Appointments completed
- Track by: Presenter, offer type, lead source

### Revenue KPIs

**Monthly Revenue:**
- Target: Based on your personal goals
- Measure: Total revenue generated
- Track by: Product type, team member, channel

**Revenue Per Lead:**
- Target: $10-50 per lead (depending on ticket size)
- Measure: Total revenue / Total leads
- Track by: Source, campaign, month

**Customer Lifetime Value:**
- Target: Based on your company's metrics
- Measure: Average total spend per customer
- Track by: Join date, source, team member

### Team KPIs

**Team Growth Rate:**
- Target: 10-20% monthly growth
- Measure: New team members / Total team
- Track by: Month, recruiter, source

**Team Member Activation Rate:**
- Target: 70-80% of new members activate within 30 days
- Measure: Activated members / New members
- Track by: Onboarding path, mentor

**Team Retention Rate:**
- Target: 80-90% monthly retention
- Measure: Active members this month / Active last month
- Track by: Tenure, activity level

## Step 7: Optimize Based on Data

### Optimization Framework

**1. Identify:** What metric needs improvement?
**2. Analyze:** Why is it underperforming?
**3. Hypothesize:** What change might improve it?
**4. Test:** Implement the change
**5. Measure:** Did it improve?
**6. Scale:** If yes, implement broadly. If no, try again.

### Common Optimization Scenarios

**Low Lead Volume:**
- Increase social media posting frequency
- Test new content types
- Optimize posting times
- Add new lead magnets
- Expand to new platforms

**Low Qualification Rate:**
- Improve targeting
- Refine qualification questions
- Update AI bot training
- Add more screening steps
- Improve lead magnets

**Low Appointment Show Rate:**
- Send better confirmations
- Add SMS reminders
- Improve appointment booking process
- Offer time slot options
- Send day-of reminders

**Low Close Rate:**
- Improve presentation skills
- Address objections earlier
- Better qualify before presenting
- Offer different entry points
- Follow up more effectively
